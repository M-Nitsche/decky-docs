A slide Decky builds from scratch costs **10 credits**. A slide built from a [Decky library](/slide-library) template costs **1 credit**. Read operations — checking status, downloading, listing, searching the library — are always free.

| What | Cost |
| --- | --- |
| Slide built from scratch ([`create_deck`](/tools/create-deck)) | 10 credits |
| Slide built from a library template ([`create_deck`](/tools/create-deck) or [`fill_templates`](/tools/fill-templates)) | 1 credit |
| AI-generated image that Decky uses in a deck | 20 credits |
| Icon that Decky uses in a deck | 1 credit |
| Web search that Decky runs while writing a deck | 1 credit |
| Status, download, lists, library search, usage | Free |

Images, icons, and web searches are charged when Decky uses them, separately from the slide reservation below. [`check_usage`](/tools/check-usage) returns the same prices in its `pricing` field.

Your credit balance belongs to your Decky AI account, so it is shared with the Decky AI PowerPoint add-in.

## How credits work

<Steps>
  <Step title="Credits are reserved upfront">
    When you call `create_deck` with 10 slides, 100 credits (10 × 10) are immediately reserved from your balance. A `fill_templates` job reserves 1 credit per slide.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Only finished slides are charged">
    When the job completes, Decky charges only for the slides it actually produced: 10 credits for each slide built from scratch, 1 credit for each slide built from a library template. Make 9 instead of 10? You're charged 90 and 10 are refunded. If 4 of the 10 came from library templates, you're charged 64 and 36 are refunded.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Canceled or failed jobs are fully refunded">
    All reserved credits come back immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Credits reset monthly">
    Your full allowance is restored at the start of each monthly period, including on yearly plans.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Example

| Action | Reserved | Charged | Balance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Start: 500 remaining | — | — | 500 |
| `create_deck` (10 slides) | 100 | — | 400 |
| Job succeeds (10 slides) | — | 100 | 400 |
| `create_deck` (5 slides) | 50 | — | 350 |
| Job canceled | — | 0 (refunded) | 400 |
| `create_deck` (10 slides) | 100 | — | 300 |
| Job succeeds (6 from scratch, 4 from the library) | — | 64 (36 refunded) | 336 |
| `fill_templates` (12 slides) | 12 | — | 324 |
| Job succeeds (11 slides filled, 1 skipped) | — | 11 (1 refunded) | 325 |

## Plans

| Plan | Monthly credits | Slides / month (from scratch) | Pricing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Free** | 200 | ~20 | Free forever |
| **Starter** | 2,000 | ~200 | Paid |
| **Pro** | 5,000 | ~500 | Paid |
| **Max** | 10,000 | ~1,000 | Paid |
| **Business / Enterprise** | Custom | Custom | Custom — contact sales |

<Note>
Paid plans are billed monthly or yearly. On either billing cycle, credits are allocated per month, and unused credits **do not roll over** to the next month. See current prices at [decky-ai.com/pricing](https://decky-ai.com/pricing).
</Note>

## Check your balance

Ask your assistant:

> "How many credits do I have left?"

Or call [`check_usage`](/tools/check-usage) directly. It returns your plan, total allocation, used amount, remaining balance, and period end date.

## Running low on credits

If you don't have enough credits for a job, `create_deck` returns:

```text
Insufficient credits: this job needs 100 (10 per slide) but only 40 remain
in the current period on the 'free' plan. Reduce the slide count or upgrade
at https://app.decky-ai.com.
```

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Reduce slides" icon="minus">
    Ask for fewer slides to fit your remaining balance, or [fill library slides](/slide-library) at 1 credit each.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Wait for reset" icon="clock">
    Credits reset at the start of the next billing period.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Upgrade" icon="arrow-up" href="https://decky-ai.com/pricing">
    Compare plans and move to a higher tier.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
