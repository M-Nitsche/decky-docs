---
title: "fill_templates"
description: "Fill a Decky library deck, or chosen library templates, with your material."
---

<Info>
**Type:** Write · **Required scope:** `decks:write` · **Cost:** 1 credit per slide, reserved upfront. Credits for slides that aren't produced are refunded automatically.
</Info>

Fills a library deck or chosen library templates from [`search_library`](/tools/search-library) with your material. This is the "Fill with AI" of the Decky PowerPoint add-in. See the [Slide library guide](/slide-library) for the whole flow.

## Parameters

<ParamField path="prompt" type="string" required>
  What the deck is for: audience, goal, key messages.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="deck_id" type="string">
  A library deck from [`search_library`](/tools/search-library) (`kind: "deck"`). Pass **either** `deck_id` **or** `template_ids`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="template_ids" type="string[]">
  Library templates from [`search_library`](/tools/search-library) (`kind: "template"`). They are filled in this order, one slide each. Maximum: **20**. Duplicate ids are ignored.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="exclude_member_ids" type="string[]">
  With `deck_id`: the ids of the deck's slides to leave out.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="context" type="string">
  Source material that the assistant extracted from the user's files: figures, tables as text, quotes, all verbatim. Up to **100,000 characters**. Decky never sees the files themselves.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="master_id" type="string">
  The master (design) to place the slides into. It works the same as in [`create_deck`](/tools/create-deck#parameters). Omit it to use the default master.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="max_slides" type="integer">
  With `deck_id` only: the most slides the deck may have. Default: the deck's slides left after `exclude_member_ids`, at most 20. Range: 1 to twice that number, at most **20**. With `template_ids`, the job always has one slide per template.
</ParamField>

## What Decky does

<Steps>
  <Step title="Plans the slides">
    **With a deck:** Decky plans the storyline over the deck's slides. It drops slides the material can't support and repeats a slide where needed (at most twice). **With templates:** they are filled in the order given.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Writes every slot">
    Text comes from your `prompt` and `context`. Charts are filled only with real data from the context. Picture slots get a picture from the Decky library, or keep the template's sample picture.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Marks what's missing">
    Where the material lacks a number, name, date, or quote, Decky writes a visible placeholder on the slide instead of inventing one, such as `[€ m — to confirm]` or `[client name — to confirm]`. Short slots get `[TBC]`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Places the slides into the master">
    The finished slides are placed into the chosen master, in order.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When the job finishes, [`get_deck_status`](/tools/get-deck-status#fill-templates-report) returns `progress.report`. It lists the slides filled, slides skipped (for example, a chart slide without usable data), the placeholders to confirm, and the facts the material lacked. Pass these on to the user.

<Warning>
If no slide can be filled at all, the job fails and all reserved credits are refunded.
</Warning>

## Response

Returns a job object with `kind: "fill_templates"`, the same shape as [`create_deck`](/tools/create-deck#response). Use the `id` to poll [`get_deck_status`](/tools/get-deck-status). It also includes:

<ResponseField name="credits_reserved" type="integer">
  Credits held for the job: 1 × `max_slides` for a deck, 1 × the number of templates for `template_ids`.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="library" type="object">
  What the job fills: `deck_id` and `exclude_member_ids`, or `template_ids`.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="fills" type="object">
  A readable summary. For a deck: `deck` (title), `deck_slides`, `excluded`, `max_slides`. For templates: `templates` (titles, in order) and `max_slides`.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="notice" type="string">
  Present when there is something to tell the user about the master the deck uses. For example, that the default was used although the user has uploaded masters.
</ResponseField>

## Errors

| Error | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Pass either deck_id … or template_ids …` | Both or neither were passed | Pass exactly one |
| `Library deck 'X' not found` | Unknown deck id | Call [`search_library`](/tools/search-library) with `kind: "deck"` |
| `Library deck '…' has no fillable template slides.` | The deck holds no template slides | Pick another deck |
| `exclude_member_ids […] are not slides of this deck` | An id isn't one of the deck's slides | Use the slide ids the error lists |
| `Every slide of the deck was excluded.` | Nothing left to fill | Exclude fewer slides |
| `max_slides must be between 1 and N for this deck.` | `max_slides` out of range | Use a value in the range given |
| `At most 20 template_ids per job.` | Too many templates | Split into several jobs |
| `Not library templates: […]` | An id isn't a library template | Use ids from [`search_library`](/tools/search-library) with `kind: "template"` |
| `context has N characters; the limit is 100000.` | Too much source material | Send the figures, tables, and quotes the deck needs, not whole documents |
| `Unknown master_id` | The master isn't available to you | Call [`list_masters`](/tools/list-masters) |
| `Insufficient credits` | Not enough credits for the slides | Fill fewer slides or [upgrade](/credits-and-plans) |
| `Too many active deck jobs` | You already have 5 jobs queued or running | Wait for one to finish, or [`cancel_deck`](/tools/cancel-deck) |
