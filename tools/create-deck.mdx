---
title: "create_deck"
description: "Queue a PowerPoint deck generation job."
---

<Info>
**Type:** Write · **Required scope:** `decks:write` · **Cost:** 10 credits per slide built from scratch, 1 credit per slide built from a library template. Reserved upfront at 10 per slide; the difference and credits for slides that aren't produced are refunded automatically.
</Info>

Decky writes the whole deck from your brief. Where a slide from its [library of designed templates](/slide-library) fits, Decky builds that slide from the template. To fill a specific library deck or chosen templates instead, use [`fill_templates`](/tools/fill-templates).

## Parameters

<ParamField path="prompt" type="string" required>
  What the deck should be about: topic, audience, tone, structure hints, key messages. The more specific, the better the result.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="slides" type="integer" required>
  Number of slides to generate. Range: **1–40**.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="master_id" type="string">
  The master template (design) from [`list_masters`](/tools/list-masters). It can be:

  - one of your uploaded masters,
  - for [work accounts](/authentication#enterprise), one of your organization's company masters,
  - one of Decky's built-in masters: Professional Blue, Graphite, Evergreen, Burgundy, or Petrol,
  - a brand master that [`list_masters`](/tools/list-masters) lists in `builtin_masters`.

  Omit it to use `default_master` from [`list_masters`](/tools/list-masters). For work accounts, that is the organization's default. Otherwise it is Decky's default, **Professional Blue**.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="context" type="string">
  Source material for the deck that the assistant extracted from the user's files (Excel, PDF, Word, notes): figures, tables as text, quotes, names, all verbatim. Up to **100,000 characters**. Decky quotes figures from it exactly and builds charts from its data. Decky never sees the files: the assistant reads them and passes what the deck needs.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="use_library" type="boolean" default="true">
  Let Decky build slides from its library of designed templates where one fits. Set it to `false` to build every slide from scratch.
</ParamField>

## Response

Returns a job object. Use the `id` to poll [`get_deck_status`](/tools/get-deck-status).

<ResponseField name="id" type="string">
  Unique job identifier.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="kind" type="string">
  `generate` for `create_deck` jobs, `fill_templates` for [`fill_templates`](/tools/fill-templates) jobs.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="status" type="string">
  One of `queued`, `running`, `succeeded`, `failed`, `canceled`.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="master_id" type="string | null">
  The master the job uses. `null` means Decky's default master.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="master" type="string | null">
  The master's name, such as `"Graphite (Decky built-in)"` or `"Professional Blue (Decky default)"`. `null` if the uploaded master has since been deleted.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="credits_reserved" type="integer">
  Credits held for this job (10 × slides).
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="credits_charged" type="integer | null">
  Final credits charged once the job settles. `null` while in progress.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="enterprise" type="boolean">
  `true` when the job runs with [enterprise data handling](/authentication#enterprise-data-handling).
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="notice" type="string">
  Present when there is something to tell the user about the master the deck uses. For example, that other built-in masters are available, or that the user has uploaded masters but none was chosen.
</ResponseField>

<Note>
In clients that show [interactive cards](/interactive-cards) (MCP Apps), such as claude.ai and Claude Desktop, the job appears as a live **deck card** with progress, a cancel button, and previews of the finished slides. See [Interactive cards](/interactive-cards#deck-card).
</Note>

## Credits

Credits are reserved at 10 per slide when the job is created. When it finishes, Decky charges:

- **10 credits** for each slide built from scratch,
- **1 credit** for each slide built from a library template (`progress.library_slides`).

The rest is refunded. For example, a 10-slide job reserves 100 credits. If 4 of its slides come from library templates, it is charged 6 × 10 + 4 × 1 = 64 credits and 36 are refunded. Failed or canceled jobs are refunded in full.

<Note>
AI-generated images, icons, and web searches that Decky uses while writing the deck are charged separately from the slide reservation: 20 credits per AI-generated image, 1 per icon, and 1 per web search. See [Credits & Plans](/credits-and-plans).
</Note>

## Job statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `queued` | Waiting to start |
| `running` | Generation in progress |
| `succeeded` | Deck is ready for download |
| `failed` | Generation failed (see `error`) |
| `canceled` | Canceled via `cancel_deck` |

## Errors

| Error | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `prompt must not be empty.` | The prompt was blank | Describe the deck |
| `slides must be between 1 and 40.` | Slide count out of range | For longer decks, create multiple jobs |
| `context has N characters; the limit is 100000.` | Too much source material | Send the figures, tables, and quotes the deck needs, not whole documents |
| `Insufficient credits` | Not enough credits for the slide count | Reduce slides or [upgrade](/credits-and-plans) |
| `Too many active deck jobs` | You already have 5 jobs queued or running | Wait for one to finish, or [`cancel_deck`](/tools/cancel-deck) |
| `Unknown master_id` | The master isn't available to your account. The error lists your uploaded and built-in masters. | Call [`list_masters`](/tools/list-masters) |

<Note>
Credits are reserved at submission time. If the job fails or is canceled, all reserved credits are refunded. Maximum 40 slides per job. For longer decks, create multiple jobs.
</Note>
