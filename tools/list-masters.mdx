---
title: "list_masters"
description: "List the master templates (designs) available to your decks, and the default one."
---

<Info>
**Type:** Read · **Required scope:** `masters:read` · **Cost:** Free
</Info>

Every deck follows a master template. This tool lists your uploaded masters, Decky's built-in masters (for [work accounts](/authentication#enterprise), led by the organization's company masters), and the default that [`create_deck`](/tools/create-deck) and [`fill_templates`](/tools/fill-templates) use when they get no `master_id`.

## Parameters

None.

## Response

<ResponseField name="masters" type="object[]">
  The masters you uploaded on the dashboard.

  <Expandable title="Fields">
    <ResponseField name="id" type="string">Pass this as `master_id` to use the master.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="name" type="string">The template's name (taken from the uploaded file's name).</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="layouts" type="string[]">The slide layout names detected in the template.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="created_at" type="string">When the template was uploaded.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="builtin_masters" type="object[]">
  Masters available without an upload, each with `id`, `name`, and `description`. In order:

  - **Work accounts:** the organization's company masters (`"… (company master)"`), then the organization's brand master when its Decky admin manages one (`"… (company brand)"`).
  - **Individual accounts:** your brand master, if you have one. That is either your own brand from the Decky add-in (`"… (your brand)"`) or a brand generated from your company email domain (`"… (company brand)"`). It is only used when you pass its `id`.
  - **Everyone:** Decky's five built-in masters, listed below.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="default_master" type="object">
  The master decks use when no `master_id` is passed: `id`, `name`, `used_when`, and `same_as` (the matching default in the Decky PowerPoint add-in). For work accounts, it is the organization's default. Otherwise, it is **Professional Blue**.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="upload_url" type="string">
  Where to upload your own masters: `https://app.decky-ai.com`.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="note" type="string">
  A hint for the assistant. Present for work accounts with an organization default, and for accounts without uploaded masters.
</ResponseField>

## Company masters (work accounts) [#company-masters]

A [work account](/authentication#enterprise) sees its organization's masters ahead of the built-ins: the company masters its Decky admin uploaded in the add-in (up to 10 per organization), then the organization's brand master if the admin manages one. Without a `master_id`, the organization's decks use its first company master, else its brand master, else Professional Blue. This matches the default in the Decky PowerPoint add-in.

<Note>
You upload your own masters on the dashboard at [app.decky-ai.com](https://app.decky-ai.com) → **Masters**, as `.pptx` or `.potx` (up to 10 per account, 30 MB each).
</Note>
